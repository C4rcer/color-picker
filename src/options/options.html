Pipette settings

Copy format

What goes on the clipboard when you click a pixel, and what the loupe shows.

The popup always offers all three, whatever you choose here.

Keyboard shortcut

Start picking on the current page: not set

While picking: click copies the color, Shift+click keeps picking, the arrow keys move one screen pixel (ten with Shift), Enter picks, Esc or a right-click cancels.

To change the shortcut, open Firefox's Add-ons and themes page (Ctrl+Shift+A), click the gear at the top and choose "Manage Extension Shortcuts".

History

0 colors saved on this device. The newest 30 are kept.

Privacy

Pipette looks at a page only after you press its button or shortcut there, and only at the part you can see. The picture it reads colors from stays in Firefox's memory until you finish picking; it is never saved or sent anywhere. Your colors and settings are stored on this device. No ads, no analytics, no account.

Support

Pipette is free, has no ads and collects no data. If it saves you time, a tip on Ko-fi keeps it maintained. A rating on Firefox Add-ons also helps other people find it.
